# Tutorial

A walkthrough on real public Illumina data is being prepared: whole-genome sequencing of *Arabidopsis
thaliana* accessions (ENA project PRJEB62038), whose plastomes RASHER extracts, assembles and compares. It
will be published here together with its report once the short-read [validation](Validation) and benchmark
are recorded.

Until then, the bundled [example](Example) walks through every feature on simulated data in a few seconds,
and [Usage](Usage) covers real-data inputs (read pairing, sample sheets, performance).

For Nanopore reads, see [BACoN](https://github.com/duceppemo/BACoN)'s potato tutorial: RASHER is its
short-read fork and the workflow is the same.
